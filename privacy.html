    Privacy Policy

    What this site keeps, why it keeps it, and how to have it removed.

    Applies to this studio site only Updated 1 November 2026 Reading time 2 minutes

    What is kept

    Contact details, message and limited enquiry-source metadata when you submit the form.

    Browser storage

    A local notice preference; no analytics or advertising cookies are configured.

    Removal

    Request access, correction or deletion by email.

    01Information we collect

    When you submit the contact form, the information sent includes your name, email address and message. The form also sends the page path, referring page (when provided by your browser), and selected campaign attribution fields (source, medium and campaign) so the studio can understand which link led to an enquiry. These details are processed by Formspree to deliver the message to the studio inbox.

    The current website build does not include configured analytics scripts, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers. This may change if the site is updated; this policy should then be updated too. Your browser stores the notice preference in local storage. If the music player is enabled in a future release, it may use session storage to remember playback position, volume and mute state for the current browser session.

    02How it is used

    The information you provide is used to reply to and manage your enquiry. The studio intends to keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to and manage the enquiry, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion from the studio. Formspree may retain or otherwise process a separate copy under its own terms, privacy policy and retention practices; a request to the studio does not automatically delete data held by that provider.

    Enquiry details and the limited attribution fields described above are used to respond to the enquiry and understand its source. They are not used to build an advertising list.

    03Sharing

    Nexus does not sell your personal information. The form provider, Formspree, receives the submitted form data to deliver and process the enquiry. The site owner may access the information needed to respond. The studio may access the enquiry to respond. WhatsApp or your email provider may process information separately if you choose to contact the studio using those links; their own privacy terms apply.

    04Your rights

    You can contact the studio to request any of the following, subject to applicable law and reasonable identity checks:

    • Send a copy of any information held about you.
    • Correct that information, or delete it completely.
    • Ask the studio to stop using your details for future replies or contact, where applicable.

    05Cookies and local storage

    This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The Accept and Decline controls store your notice choice as a key in browser local storage; this is browser storage, not a cookie. These controls only record your preference about dismissing the notice; they do not enable analytics or advertising. You can remove it by clearing this site's local storage in your browser settings.

    If you arrive through a link containing campaign parameters, selected values (source, medium and campaign) and the landing-page path are added to the contact form. The referring page may also be included when the browser supplies it. These fields are sent only when you submit the form. The current music player, when enabled, uses session storage for playback state during that browser session.

    06Contact

    Questions about this policy can be sent to vr.nexus01@gmail.com or +91 80779 29716. The WhatsApp line is open 10:00 to 18:00 IST, Monday to Friday.
