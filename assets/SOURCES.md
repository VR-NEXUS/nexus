# Where the three typography plates come from

The round 72 specimen sheets carry one photograph each, cut to the shape of the
frame (260 by 1368) so nothing is trimmed at run time. All three are free of
attribution requirements. Nothing here is reused from elsewhere on the site.

```
type-metal   V&A / Unsplash via Wikimedia Commons, "Weldon Spring letterpress
             (Unsplash).jpg" - CC0 1.0
type-case    "Historic Cherokee printing press letters at New Echota in Gordon
             County, Georgia" - public domain
type-press   "A corner of the stamp branch, Stamp Printing Branch, Government
             Print Office, ca. 1910" - public domain
```

The twelve frames inside the process panels (assets/step-1a ... step-4c) are
twelve separate photographs, cut 4:3 at 600 by 450 and graded down into the
site's palette. Every one is CC0, from the photographs Unsplash released into
the public domain and Wikimedia Commons hosts, so no credit line is required:
